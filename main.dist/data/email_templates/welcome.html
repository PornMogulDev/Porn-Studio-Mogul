Welcome to Porn Studio Mogul!

Thank you for trying the game. Expect it to be very unfinished and hopefully only somewhat broken.

Here are a few tips to get started:

  • Plan a Shooting Block from the Schedule tab to create Scenes.
  • Double-click said Scenes to open the Scene Planner. Lay out everything in the scene and move it to its Casting phase.
  • Go to the Talent tab and manually search or use the filtering options to start casting actors for your scenes. Double-clicking their table row opens their Talent Profile, from where you can hire them.

The Help feature is not very well-implemented at the moment, but you might read it to get a better idea of how things are working at this time and what I've been thinking about them.

You might also get that if you visit the available sites, where I'll also be happy to answer your questions, feedback, suggestions...

Thanks again!
