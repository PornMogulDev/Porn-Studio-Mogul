This project is a turn-based, text-driven management and business simulation game. Its primary inspirations include Total Extreme Wrestling, Football Manager, Hollywood Mogul, Free Cities, and Paradox grand-strategy titles.

The player’s role is similar to a producer or studio manager. Their core responsibilities include:

Planning, designing, and scheduling scenes (from pre-production to release)

Hiring and managing talent

Responding to dynamic events, ranging from one-screen prompts to multi-week story developments

Making strategic choices that influence finances, studio reputation, talent relationships, and long-term narrative direction

The game generates substantial data—talent profiles, scene records, market statistics—which is intentionally exposed to the player to support analysis, planning, and emergent storytelling.

A key design goal is to simulate the adult-film industry in a grounded, systemic way rather than applying a simple management-game template. Important industry characteristics include:

A highly fluid talent pool with frequent turnover (many careers last under two years)

Cross-role mobility, where individuals may take on multiple responsibilities (e.g., actor, director, producer, camera operator)

Market segmentation, where viewer groups have distinct tastes, purchasing power, demographics, and market shares that influence both demand and revenue

The world is designed to feel dynamic and reactive. Over time, numerous variables evolve, including:

Talent skills, attributes, psychology, and relationships

Viewer-group preferences, spending behavior, and market share

Rival studios’ performance and market presence

Economic and cultural trends that shape demand and opportunities

These interacting systems allow for rich emergent narratives that reflect both player decisions and autonomous world simulation.
